Take a quiz inside Repformed

  1. Open the assigned week and choose Take quiz or Continue quiz.
  2. Answer on the website or app. Choose Save draft before leaving and wait for confirmation. A failed or offline save is not a saved draft.
  3. Choose Submit answers when ready. Multiple-choice and true/false answers are scored by the server. Written answers wait for staff grading.
  4. Open Progress to see results and feedback. A retry is a new attempt; earlier attempts stay in history.

A saved template or new quiz version never changes a previously released week or its grades. If access ends, recorded progress and grades remain in history; protected training access follows the account’s eligibility period.

Staff: build once, enroll from the athlete

  1. In Training, upload lessons and send them through review. An upload is not a release. Only complete approved content can go into a ready week.
  2. Open Weekly plans & quizzes. Create the reusable plan, add lessons and imported quiz versions to each week, then approve the finished weeks. Week 0 is optional orientation.
  3. In Athletes, search or filter the paged roster and open the athlete. An Owner records verified package eligibility and its access window, then chooses Start a weekly plan. Enrollment starts that athlete’s own week 1.
  4. For a cohort, open the training bank’s Groups tab. Select athletes across pages, save the group, review its plan and confirm enrollment. New members must be eligible. Existing matching plans are kept without resetting weeks or grades; a conflicting running plan blocks the group operation until resolved.
  5. Use each athlete’s plan controls for pause, resume, repeat or a future-week override. Repeating makes a fresh practice copy; ending stops future releases. Earlier work and grades remain in history.

Manual eligibility is not proof of payment. Changing an assigned service coach does not change account roles, billing or private video-review access. Advanced legacy single-workout assignment is separate from weekly enrollment.

Who can do what?
  • Owners: enrollment, eligibility, groups and delivery-rule defaults; all client service records.
  • Managers: all client service records, assigned-coach changes and week approval; enrollment controls remain Owner-only.
  • Coaches: assigned athletes, their necessary learning results and written grading; authoring stays within permitted content scope.
  • Parents: their own family profiles, permitted practice review and learning history.

Upload quizzes from Google Sheets

No quiz-writing studio or Google account connection is required. Keep the spreadsheet and real answer keys private to staff.

  1. Download the blank CSV template or fictional format example, then open it in Google Sheets.
  2. Keep the exact header row and use one question per row.
  3. Choose File → Download → Comma-separated values (.csv).
  4. In Quiz uploads, choose a new quiz or a new version of an existing quiz, select the file, review questions and keys, then save the version. Fix validation errors and upload again; malformed files never partially publish.
  5. Add the saved version to future plan weeks explicitly. Existing released versions and attempts stay unchanged.

question_id,order,type,question,option_a,option_b,option_c,option_d,correct_answer,points,explanation

Types: multiple_choice, true_false, written. Keys: A–D, TRUE/FALSE, or blank for written responses. Use consecutive order numbers, unique question IDs, whole-number points from 1–100, and 1–100 questions per UTF-8 CSV, up to 256 KB.

Staff grade written answers from the athlete’s quiz history. Each grade correction is a new revision; the previous grade is retained. Group results show only athletes within the logged-in staff member’s scope.

Know what needs attention

Friday reminders: in-app reminders apply to unfinished current-week work. The team sets the reminder time. Paused/inactive work, completed work and old weeks do not create new Friday nags; a linked athlete with independent recording paused is not reminded to do work they cannot record.

Staff content-capacity alerts: the queue checks consecutive approved ready weeks. Open the named athlete from an alert, check their next release and prepare the missing future week. Draft uploads do not count as ready content. Marking an alert read does not fix the missing content.

Routine training and Friday reminders are in-app. This guide does not enable email, device push or a standing scheduler. Required sign-in and password-reset email remains separate.
