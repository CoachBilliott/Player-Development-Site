Account setup and deletion.

A private invitation opens the right workspace. Customer deletion includes the login and owned training data. Staff and guardian-managed accounts use a review so their responsibilities can be handled safely.

Owners: invite an adult.

  1. Open Account or Accounts & training access, then Invite someone to Repformed.
  2. Enter the adult’s email and choose Parent, Athlete, Coach or Manager. Invite only adults who requested access. New Owner access is assigned later through the existing account-level controls after verified setup.
  3. Select Review invitation, check the email and intended level, then Confirm & request invitation email. Keep editing invitation cancels without sending.
  4. Refresh Recent invitations. Awaiting recipient means the email service accepted the request, not that the recipient received or accepted it. Check failures before retrying; failed requests wait at least 60 seconds. A dedicated sender is needed for reliable customer delivery.
  5. For a verified email without completed account access, the Owner can prepare its setup invitation without another email. That person signs in normally and accepts the approved setup. Existing configured accounts use Manage account access.
Separate permissions

An invitation does not assign training, charge money or provide Apple TestFlight access. TestFlight tester access is separate. Cancelling an invitation prevents its access from activating even if an email is already in transit.

Recipients: finish your own setup.

  1. Open the invitation email and follow its verification/password steps on the website. Choose your own Repformed password: at least 8 characters, uppercase, lowercase, number and special character.
  2. Confirm you are 18 or older and read the adult pilot privacy/access information. Accept the specific level selected by your Owner. Staff do not choose Parent or Athlete to enter their management workspace.
  3. Sign in to the iPhone app with that same Repformed account. Your Apple Account and Repformed password are separate.

Cancelled or expired invitations cannot complete setup. Email links follow the email provider’s expiry, which may be shorter than the seven-day access invitation. Ask an Owner to check access; use website password recovery in Safari if needed.

Customers: review full account deletion.

  1. Open Account → Delete your account → Review account deletion. Check the email, profile count and private clip count.
  2. Re-enter your current Repformed password and type DELETE. Keep my account closes this review without starting deletion.
  3. Select Delete my account permanently. This starts irreversible cleanup: private stored clips, owned profiles/history, saved practice, family links and assignments, then the email login.
  4. Keep the app open until the result is confirmed. If cleanup fails, access pauses and the same request remains retryable. Use Continue account deletion; closing its dialog does not cancel work already started.
Other people’s accounts stay intact

A parent’s deletion removes their owned household profiles and clips, but does not delete a linked adult’s independent login. A linked adult’s deletion removes their own uploads and connection; the other household keeps its own profile/history with the reporter identity cleared.

Staff and guardian-managed closure.

Choose Request assisted account closure, review the explanation, type DELETE, then Confirm closure review request. This creates a request; it does not delete the account automatically.

Owners must arrange account ownership before closing. Staff training-content ownership needs review. Guardian-managed access requires its verified review process. An Owner sees requests in Account → Account closure requests. Mark request reviewed records that review only; it does not claim the account was erased.

What can remain?

Other households retain their own records with your reporter identity cleared. A minimal operational receipt retains the request ID, status, counts and dates without your email. Provider backups, security records and copies someone already downloaded follow their applicable retention rules; deleting an account does not erase those copies.

Private practice clips are otherwise retained until an authorized user deletes them; there is no automatic video expiry schedule. The final public-launch privacy, retention and support policies still need review. This guide explains current pilot controls and is not a final legal policy.

Keep the pilot private.

Use approved adult accounts and original or licensed, qualified-reviewed training content. Public signup, billing and real junior enrollment/media remain closed. Training and parent reports describe participation, not proof of improved athletic ability.
