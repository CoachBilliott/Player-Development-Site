Your interest. Your information.

This notice covers the adult prelaunch interest form. Expressing interest does not create an account, enroll an athlete, reserve a place or make a payment.

What the form collects

Your adult email, package interest and optional adult name, together with the request reference and submission time. If you use the optional launch-email checkbox, we also record your choice, the notice shown and its version, and the time. The form asks you to declare that you are 18 or older. Do not enter a child’s name, birth date, health information, videos or other child information.

How it is used and stored

Repformed saves these details in its private database, hosted by Supabase, so authorized Owners and Managers can review and respond to your interest. Coaches and other customers cannot view this interest inbox. Submitting interest does not add you to a marketing mailing list or send an automatic email to you.

Your optional launch-email choice

“Email me when Repformed launches” starts unchecked. You can submit the form without choosing it. Checking it records a separate request for a launch email; this form does not send a launch announcement. It does not verify that you own the email address.

Leaving it unchecked adds no permission and does not cancel an earlier affirmative request. Separate submissions retain their preference history; a retry does not create another preference record. Contact us to change or withdraw an earlier request. Existing interest requests are not treated as launch-email permission.

If enabled, staff alerts go only to Repformed@gmail.com through Resend. The alert links staff to the private workspace and does not include your name or contact email. If staff email delivery fails, your saved request remains available for staff review.

Questions and requests

Contact Repformed@gmail.com about your interest information or to request access, correction or removal. Removal is handled by the team; this page does not promise an automated deletion schedule.
