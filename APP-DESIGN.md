# Private app design

The native app and `?source=app` browser preview share the same app surfaces. The public website and its single fictional demo remain separate.

- Staff open directly into their authorized workspace. The home screen prioritizes **Build a workout** and **Upload a drill**. Content bank, video reviews and Owner-only access controls use compact tool rows. Account and Help remain in navigation.
- Customers open directly into **Today** after sign-in. A compact athlete switcher leads into one next-session surface. Session name, preparation and the start action come first. **Why this session?** expands the matching explanation. Workouts, Progress and Account retain their distinct jobs.
- Content opens the file picker before extended help. Publishing destination, refresh and sign-out are available under **Publishing options**. Rights, visibility, preview and publish confirmation remain in the upload flow. Device draft limits remain explicit.
- Graphite surfaces, readable typography, restrained orange accents, licensed field photography and a floating bottom navigation replace nested website dashboard cards. Brief entrance motion follows the device's reduced-motion preference.
- Protected routes, private profile isolation, junior enrollment gates, parent permissions, workout snapshots and optional review access are unchanged. No payments, new enrollment or security changes are introduced.

## Device acceptance

Check the new build in TestFlight on actual iPhones: text sizing and safe areas; one-hand tab navigation; sign-in/relaunch; start/next/back/end practice; family switching and approvals; file picker and device drafts; camera/video selection, preview, send and delete. Browser and simulator checks do not replace physical-device testing or qualified content review.
