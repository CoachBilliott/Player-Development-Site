# Player Development — two-phone test

Allow 15–20 minutes. Use the latest TestFlight build on both iPhones. This is a private pilot acceptance test, not proof of athletic improvement or public-launch approval.

## Before you start

- Keep one phone signed in as Owner. On the other, use a separate approved adult Parent or Athlete pilot account. An Owner account does not become a customer account. If that second account is missing, complete the staff checks and request provisioning with the adult's email; do not share passwords.
- Use one original 15–30 second adult/noncontact recording and fictional player nicknames. Confirm media rights and qualified coaching review before treating it as real training. Real junior enrollment and junior video uploads remain closed.
- Note each phone model, iOS version and the TestFlight build number. Keep original recordings in Photos.

## 1. Choose, prepare and upload a video

On the Owner phone, open Content → Choose & prepare iPhone video. Cancel the picker once: no draft or upload should be created. Choose the original clip, shorten its start/end if needed, and prepare it. Confirm MP4 playback, orientation and audio in the preview; the original remains in Photos.

Add a clear title, purpose, equipment, practice steps, coaching cue and intended audience. Keep access on Protected training. Save a device draft, leave and return, then restore it. Confirm rights again. Preview and choose Save shared draft. Watch the transfer indicator and wait for confirmation.

**Pass:** one private shared draft; the other phone cannot train from it yet. A canceled preparation or rejected replacement keeps the previously selected valid file. A failed transfer keeps the draft for retry. Local device drafts do not sync to the other phone.

## 2. Review and release the drill

Open Content → Drafts & approvals. Preview the exact file, instructions and audience. Send for review. As Owner or Manager, request one small change with a note. Edit the draft, save and resubmit. Preview again, acknowledge the release checks and approve it.

**Pass:** stages change from Draft → Awaiting review → Changes requested → Awaiting review → Approved. Coaches can submit their own drafts but cannot approve. Public samples stay private until approval. Staff approval does not replace qualified coaching review.

## 3. Build and assign the complete workout

On the approved protected drill, choose Build a workout with this drill. Confirm it appears in the practice order. Add a second approved matching drill if available; move the order once. Preview and save the workout. Choose the approved adult customer account, preview eligibility, then confirm the assignment.

Refresh Today on the customer phone. **Pass:** the named workout has the same complete drill order, equipment and instructions. An incompatible athlete does not receive a partial workout. Assigning training does not enable payments or video-review access.

## 4. Pause, resume and finish practice

On the customer phone, start the workout and review the practice acknowledgement. Complete the first drill if the workout has two, then choose Save & leave workout. Close/reopen the app and resume. Check the saved drill. Repeat once with a page/app refresh. Confirm there is no completion in Progress yet.

Try discarding saved practice, cancel that confirmation, then resume and finish. Tap Finish twice. **Pass:** the saved position survives; cancel keeps it; exactly one completion appears. Watching a video or pausing never counts as completion. If offline or instructions/access changed, saving/continuing must show a clear error rather than claim success. Reconnect and refresh. Instruction changes can require a newly approved/reassigned workout.

## 5. Check optional review and both devices

Only if an Owner separately enabled adult pilot video review and assigned a coach: record/choose a short adult clip, cancel once, prepare/preview it, then send. Verify the assigned coach can play it and save feedback. Check the customer can read that feedback after reopening. Cancel deletion once, then delete the test clip. Confirm it no longer plays from a newly opened review.

On both phones check large text, VoiceOver labels, keyboard visibility, portrait/landscape video and comfortable tap targets. Sign out and verify private training does not remain visible. A different account must not see the previous account's saved practice.

**Pass:** capture, upload, playback and deletion work on actual hardware; private access stays restricted. Browser/simulator tests alone do not establish this result. A signed media link already issued can remain valid until its short expiry.

## Tell us what needs adjusting

For each issue record: phone/iOS/build; screen; what you tapped; expected result; actual result; whether it repeats. Use screenshots or adult-only recordings with credentials and private details hidden. Do not include real children's information.

Decision after testing: ready for the approved adult pilot, needs fixes, or blocked by a named requirement. Public registration, billing, real junior access and public App Store release require their separate launch work.
