# Contact and public help

The website Contact page is `#contact`, linked from public desktop/mobile navigation and the footer. The app help source also links to the public Contact page. The published contact address is **Repformed@gmail.com**, explicitly supplied for publication by the Owner. It does not rename the platform or change authentication settings.

`src/contactContent.ts` owns the address, supported message topics and authored FAQ answers. Changing an email topic only prepares an encoded `mailto:` subject; **Open email app** opens the visitor's email client. The visitor writes and sends there. The website does not submit, store or claim delivery of a contact message. No SMTP service or email credentials are needed for that link, and no test emails are sent by browser checks.

FAQ search runs in the browser and does not send queries to an AI provider. **Quick help**, available from the website footer, Contact page and app help, uses those same approved answers in a focused dialog. Visitors can choose a topic, search, open its next step or prepare an email to the team. Closing the dialog clears its search and selection. No conversation or query is stored or transmitted. Unmatched questions hand off to the real inbox without inventing an answer or claiming a message was sent.

Answers describe current registration, payment, junior-access and video-review limits. Keep those answers current as features launch. The dialog explicitly identifies itself as FAQ help. **Live AI is not enabled**: no API credentials or paid provider were supplied. Before activation, use a server-side key, approved FAQ grounding, strict output limits, request/cost controls, clear AI identification and a human handoff. Keep private account data out of public AI context. A ChatGPT subscription does not supply API usage. Do not place API keys in public configuration or source control.

Official social links share `public/social-links.json` across the website footer, Contact page, app help and the online partner/account/phone-test guides. See [SOCIAL-LINKS.md](./SOCIAL-LINKS.md). The Owner verified Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and X; the public Repformed YouTube channel URL is also verified. All five are published through the shared configuration.

Public signup, real junior enrollment and paid checkout are unchanged. The website email link does not configure automatic invitations, verification/reset messages or review notifications; those still require the separate sending setup. Published changes to app help become available in the native binary only in a subsequent app release.
