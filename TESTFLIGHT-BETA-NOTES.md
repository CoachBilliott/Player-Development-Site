## Beta app description

Player Development is an early football-training pilot for individual athletes and parent-managed families. Approved adult pilot accounts sign in to a private workspace, practice assigned training and record participation. Staff publish protected instructional content and specifically assigned coaches can review optional private adult practice videos. A separate sample-data customer demo remains on the public website. Football is the current program; future sports have not been announced.

All built-in sample activities are illustrative and pending qualified coaching review. Practice reports and parent reviews describe participation, not proof of improved athletic ability. The beta does not offer medical advice, guaranteed recruiting outcomes, live payments, public player profiles or direct messaging. Real junior enrollment is closed.

## What to test

Install and launch the app, sign in with an approved adult pilot account, practice an assigned workout and check submitted participation after closing/reopening. Test the drill order, instructions, media, next/previous actions and canceled unfinished practice. Authorized staff can check shared publishing, device draft save/restore and content editing. Private account/training access requires a connection; it is not an offline sample catalog. Please report the screen, iPhone/iOS version, steps, expected behavior and actual result, using fictional information only.

## Reviewer instructions

Owners, Managers and Coaches open staff account settings directly without Parent/Athlete enrollment. The native app opens private account login; the public sample demo remains on the website. No sample accounts, demo records or built-in demo drills are inserted into real customer plans.

1. Sign in using an approved, verified adult review account supplied privately in App Store Connect. These credentials must be prepared before requesting external beta review; none are invented in this document.
2. Open Today, begin the assigned workout and confirm each drill shows its attached demonstration and all practice steps together. Complete practice before tapping Complete drill / Finish workout. Reports describe participation, not measured ability.
3. With optional pilot review access enabled for the adult review account, choose Send a drill video, record or select a short adult/fictional test clip, preview and send. Canceling selection must not upload anything. Check submission status and any privately saved coaching feedback.
4. Authorized staff can use Reviews for accounts specifically assigned to them. No staff role automatically exposes family profiles or clips. Real junior recording remains closed.

Review clips are stored privately until an authorized family/uploader deletes them. The pilot has no automated retention schedule, live payments or public registration. Include these behaviors accurately in review/privacy disclosures.
